# Changelog

- **2026-10-31:** Initial web and app rules, built from a review of ~40 projects and Spencer's past Claude/Codex design feedback, then corrected by Spencer.
- **2026-11-06:** Added two bans to the web rules (apps inherit them): features that do nothing for the user, and orphaned controls, plus a checklist line. From Spencer, after a Google Analytics card in UTern Settings outlived the popup it belonged to.
- **2026-11-07:** White is the standard background, also on devices in dark mode; dark mode is opt-in from settings (web 2, app 8). Added two standard screens (web 5e): sign in and sign up ("Open Page": no card, no tab toggle, soft-filled fields, text links), and the opportunity/item sheet ("Facts First": four facts, About open and readable, the rest folded). From Spencer, on UTern.
- **2026-11-11:** Added the people video ("A Person And A Message", web 8a): a six-second clip of one person in their day with the assistant's messages over it as glass bubbles, one wide clip under a short headline and one per feature. From Spencer, on the UTern landing ("I love the style of video you made. Save that into the global design rules file").
- **2026-11-12:** Web 6: headlines of about four words over a visual, a printed number carries its source and assumptions, copy names everyone the product covers. App 6: never an empty screen over data you have, tools above the work, one continuous nav. App 10: list views and a stage path for enterprise records. From Spencer, on UTern ("Let's add the rest of the design rules too").
- **2026-11-15:** Web 8b: redesigns and mockups keep and upgrade the client's own images and videos, so the new version never looks bare and looks clearly better than the original. Upscale low-res photos with existing Higgsfield credits or a local sharpen; never invent photos of the business, staff or work; don't caption staff with unconfirmed names. From Spencer, on SparkTech mockups ("When designing websites always keep the images and videos in the new version it shouldn't look bare"; "It should look even better").
- **2026-11-17:** Web 4: a button that shares a pill with an input fills the field, full height and flush in its end, with no ring of the field showing around it. From Spencer, on the BallotWatch ZIP lookup ("Dont have whitespace around the button like this. Add this to design rules"). Web 5: developer features (API, MCP server, SDKs, open data) get one "For developers" page linked in the nav and footer, never a landing-page section.
- **2026-11-21:** From Spencer, on SparkTech. Web 2: the accent is the favicon's exact color (logo, accent and every button match the browser-tab icon); SparkTech gold is now `#ffd15c`. Web 4: buttons look finished on dark grounds (a soft top edge and own-color glow on the primary pill, clear glass for the secondary). Web 4a: a price or plan shown as a card is a real membership card that turns toward the pointer while its lighting stays fixed ("I want the card to actually move, not the light"), the one exception to the gimmick-motion ban. Web 5: every FAQ is a native dropdown with FAQPage data ("it should always be like that"); the footer has no CTA or tagline ("Don't put a CTA in the footer"), Legal is its own column, every page appears in it, and the bottom shows only the logo and ©. Checklist updated to match.
